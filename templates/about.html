My perspective

Geography is both
a lens and a method.

My master’s studies are building a foundation in GIS, remote sensing, and spatial data science. I’m especially interested in the point where rigorous processing becomes a clear visual explanation.

This site documents that learning process. The demonstrations are intentionally transparent about their inputs and limits; as my coursework develops, the portfolio will grow to include new analyses, research questions, and places.

My approach

Good maps start
with good questions.

I care about the full path from raw data to visual story: choosing suitable inputs, understanding their limits, and making the result legible.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Start with a place, a question, and data whose resolution and provenance fit the task.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Build transparent spatial workflows in Python, with explicit CRS handling and documented decisions.

  3. 03

    Communicate

    Use cartography to reveal the pattern without hiding uncertainty or overstating the result.
